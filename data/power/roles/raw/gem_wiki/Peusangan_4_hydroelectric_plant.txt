'''Other names:''' Peusangan IV hydroelectric plant

'''Peusangan 4 hydroelectric plant''' is a hydroelectric power plant in pre-construction in Takengon Timur Village, Lut Tawar District, Central Aceh Regency, Aceh Province, Indonesia.
