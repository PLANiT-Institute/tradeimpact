'''Lower Palas Valley hydroelectric plant''' (Chor Nullah hydroelectric plant) is a hydroelectric power plant in pre-construction in Pattan Town, Pattan Tehsil, Lower Kohistan District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.
