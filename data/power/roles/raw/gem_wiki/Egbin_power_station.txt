==Background==

The plant is the largest power generating station in Nigeria.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egbin_Thermal_Power_Station|title=Egbin Thermal Power Station|last=|first=|date=|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref> 

'''In 1982''', construction of the plant commenced.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egbin_Thermal_Power_Station|title=Egbin Thermal Power Station|last=|first=|date=|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://globalenergyobservatory.org/geoid/42567|title=Egbin Thermal Power Plant Nigeria - GEO|website=globalenergyobservatory.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref>

'''In July 1985''', the first unit was commissioned.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://globalenergyobservatory.org/geoid/42567|title=Egbin Thermal Power Plant Nigeria - GEO|website=globalenergyobservatory.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref>

'''In November 1986''', the sixth unit was commissioned.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://globalenergyobservatory.org/geoid/42567|title=Egbin Thermal Power Plant Nigeria - GEO|website=globalenergyobservatory.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref> 

'''In 2006''', unit six of the plant suffered a boiler tube explosion. Its rehabilitation lasted 3 months and was completed in January 2015.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.vanguardngr.com/2015/01/egbin-power-plant-rehabilitated-produce-220mw/|title=Egbin power plant rehabilitated, to produce 220MW|last=Eboh|first=Michael|date=19 January 2015|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref> 

'''In 2007''', KEPCO Energy Resources offered to pay US $280 million to acquire 51 percent of the power plant.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ppi.worldbank.org/en/snapshots/project/egbin-power-plant-4858|title=Private Participation in Infrastructure (PPI) - World Bank Group|website=ppi.worldbank.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref> KEPCO paid 10% of the bid amount to express interest.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150211111315/http://nigeriapoliticsonline.com/korean-firm-wins-bid-for-egbin-power-plant/|title=Korean firm wins bid for Egbin Power plant {{!}} Nigeria Politics Online|date=2015-02-11|website=web.archive.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref> However, as a result of power purchase and gas supply agreement issues, the privatization process was halted.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150211111315/http://nigeriapoliticsonline.com/korean-firm-wins-bid-for-egbin-power-plant/|title=Korean firm wins bid for Egbin Power plant {{!}} Nigeria Politics Online|date=2015-02-11|website=web.archive.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref>

'''In November 2013''', 70% of the company was acquired by KEPCO at US $407.3 million. The other 30% is held by the Government of Nigeria.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/ozabs-us-nigeria-power-kepco-idAFKBN0TZ1MN20151216|title=Nigeria's Egbin Power plans to double generating capacity - CEO|last=Ohuocha|first=Chijioke|date=2015-12-16|work=Reuters|access-date=2022-07-11|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.usaid.gov/powerafrica/nigeria|title=Power Africa in Nigeria {{!}} Fact Sheet {{!}} Power Africa {{!}} U.S. Agency for International Development|date=2022-04-05|website=www.usaid.gov|language=en|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150211111315/http://nigeriapoliticsonline.com/korean-firm-wins-bid-for-egbin-power-plant/|title=Korean firm wins bid for Egbin Power plant {{!}} Nigeria Politics Online|date=2015-02-11|website=web.archive.org|access-date=2022-07-11}}</ref>

Egbin Power Plc plans to increase its generation capacity to 2,640 MW over three to four years at a cost of US $1.8 billion.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/ozabs-us-nigeria-power-kepco-idAFKBN0TZ1MN20151216|title=Nigeria's Egbin Power plans to double generating capacity - CEO|last=Ohuocha|first=Chijioke|date=2015-12-16|work=Reuters|access-date=2022-07-11|language=en}}</ref> The project is expected to help meet the growing energy demand. 

'''In December 2015''', Reuters reported that the company had talks with Exim Bank, the World Bank and African Development Bank were ongoing to fund the Phase II expansion plan.<ref name=":3">{{Cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/ozabs-us-nigeria-power-kepco-idAFKBN0TZ1MN20151216|title=Nigeria's Egbin Power plans to double generating capacity - CEO|last=Ohuocha|first=Chijioke|date=2015-12-16|work=Reuters|access-date=2022-07-11|language=en}}</ref> The company spent US $250 million to commission engineering analysis and evacuation studies for transmission.<ref name=":3">{{Cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/ozabs-us-nigeria-power-kepco-idAFKBN0TZ1MN20151216|title=Nigeria's Egbin Power plans to double generating capacity - CEO|last=Ohuocha|first=Chijioke|date=2015-12-16|work=Reuters|access-date=2022-07-11|language=en}}</ref>

'''In March 2016''', it was announced that the plant may be closed due to financial strains.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.economist.com/middle-east-and-africa/2016/03/03/powerless|title=Powerless|work=The Economist|access-date=2022-07-11|issn=0013-0613}}</ref>

'''In December 2017''', the plant commenced commercial operations.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.economist.com/middle-east-and-africa/2016/03/03/powerless|title=Powerless|work=The Economist|access-date=2022-07-11|issn=0013-0613}}</ref>

'''In 2022''', Egbin Power intends to begin the first phase of the expansion project starting with the construction of open-cycle turbines.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|url=https://www.power-technology.com/news/nigeria-egbin-power/|title=Egbin Power plans to raise $1.8bn to increase power capacity|last=|first=|date=|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref>

'''In 2025''', Egbin expects to finish the construction of closed-cycle units.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|url=https://www.power-technology.com/news/nigeria-egbin-power/|title=Egbin Power plans to raise $1.8bn to increase power capacity|last=|first=|date=|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref>
