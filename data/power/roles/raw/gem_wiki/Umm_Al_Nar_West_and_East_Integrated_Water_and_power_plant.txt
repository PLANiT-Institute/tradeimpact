'''Umm Al Nar West and East Integrated Water and power plant''' (محطة مجمع أم النار للطاقة, محطة أم النار للكهرباء والماء , محطة أم النار الغربية للطاقة) is an operating power station of at least 1706-megawatts (MW) in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as UAN (Sas Al Nakhl) cogeneration CCGT (Unit CC), Umm Al Nar Gas Thermal IWPP Project (Unit CC), Sas Al Nakheel (SASN) power station (Unit CC), New Umm Al Nar plant (Unit CC), UAN East (Units GT01, GT02, ST1, ST2, ST3, ST4, ST5, ST6, ST7, ST8), Umm Al Nar East plant (Units GT01, GT02, ST1, ST2, ST3, ST4, ST5, ST6, ST7, ST8), Umm Al Nar West cogeneration plant (Units GT01, GT02, ST1, ST2, ST3, ST4, ST5, ST6, ST7, ST8).
