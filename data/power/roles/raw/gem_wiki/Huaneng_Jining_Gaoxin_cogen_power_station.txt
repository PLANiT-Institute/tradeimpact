'''Huaneng Jining Gaoxin cogen power station''' (华能济宁高新区热电) is an operating power station of at least 700-megawatts (MW) in Sunshidian Village, Yanzhou District, Jining, Shandong, China with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
