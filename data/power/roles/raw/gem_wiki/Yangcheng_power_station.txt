'''Yangcheng power station''' (山西阳城发电厂) is an operating power station of at least 3300-megawatts (MW) in Beiliu, Yangcheng, Jincheng, Shanxi, China with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as 大唐阳城电厂.

==Background==

Yangcheng power station is an eight-unit coal-fired power station totaling 3,300 MW. It was built in 2001 to 2007, and is majority-owned by [[China Datang]].<ref>[http://baike.baidu.com/view/3291737.htm "阳城电厂,"] Baidu, accessed Nov 2016</ref>

The plant is the site of the world's two largest dry [[cooling tower]]s for its 2×600MW phase 2 units.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vag-group.com/Resources/Common/References/Ref-Pro_Yangcheng_edition02-29-08-2013_EN_web.pdf|title=Yangcheng Coal-Fired Power Plant|date=2018-06-11|access-date=2021-01-02}}</ref>

In November 2023, a 2 x 1,000 MW Phase III was announced at Yangcheng power station.<ref>[https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/qX6PKnFwHESydG68Mk8mlg 大唐阳城电厂三期2×1000MW煤电项目调度会顺利召开,] mp.weixin.qq.com, November 22, 2023</ref>

Phase III Unit 9 had project coordination meetings held in November 2023<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/qX6PKnFwHESydG68Mk8mlg|title=大唐阳城电厂三期2×1000MW煤电项目调度会|date=2023-11-20|website=微信公众平台|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref> and the social stability risk assessment was publicized<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.yczf.gov.cn/zwgk/gsgg/202311/t20231119_1888550.shtml|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目社会稳定风险评估公示|date=2023-11-19|website=阳城县人民政府|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. In December 2023, a project promotion meeting was held<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://news.bjx.com.cn/html/20231221/1351438.shtml|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目工作推进会议|date=2023-12-21|website=北极星电力网|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. In February 2024, another project promotion meeting was held<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://news.bjx.com.cn/html/20240304/1363927.shtml|title=阳泉县召开大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目推进会|date=2024-03-04|website=北极星电力网|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. No news emerged for several months. A further meeting was held in November 2024 to push for project approval<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.yczf.gov.cn/xwdt/ycxw/202411/t20241126_2066943.shtml|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目推进会|date=2024-11-26|website=阳城县人民政府|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. In 2025, a transmission planning study was tendered in March<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://news.bjx.com.cn/html/20250303/1429808.shtml|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目输电规划研究招标|date=2025-03-03|website=北极星电力网|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref> and a project promotion meeting was held in July<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://news.bjx.com.cn/html/20250714/1451001.shtml|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目推进会召开|date=2025-07-14|website=北极星电力网|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. The EIA was publicly notified in October<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/VQ8bTgpv0w3I8sBdrBCsRw|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目EIA受理公示|date=2025-12-08|website=微信公众平台|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>. In February 2026, the EIA was approved by the provincial authorities<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/diWFEa4PhchYtApjjzw5Ag|title=大唐阳城2×1000MW煤电项目EIA审批决定公告|date=2026-04-10|website=微信公众平台|url-status=live|access-date=2026-07-29}}</ref>.
