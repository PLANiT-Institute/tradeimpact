==Background==

The Litos project's 2020 environmental impact report ([http://licenciamento.ibama.gov.br/Termeletricas/UTE%20s%20%20%20%20LITOS/ RIMA]) estimated construction start dates of 2021 for Litos 1, 2023 for [[Litos 2 power station|Litos 2]], and 2025 for [[Litos 3 power station|Litos 3]] and [[Litos 4 power station|Litos 4]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://litosenergia.com.br/RIMA_Litos.pdf|title=RIMA (Relatório de Impacto Ambiental)|last=|first=|date=June 2020|website=Oiti Consultoria Ambiental|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref> A public hearing for the Litos power stations was held on September 30, 2020 to address concerns raised by the report.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://audienciaspublicas.org/cases/audiencia-publica-virtual-sobre-o-projeto-das-usinas-termeletricas-litos-1-2-3-e-4/|title=Audiência Pública Virtual sobre o projeto das Usinas Termelétricas Litos 1, 2, 3 e 4|last=|first=|date=|work=UPtime / Audiências Públicas|access-date=2023-10-04|language=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.gov.br/ibama/pt-br/assuntos/notas/2020/ibama-realiza-audiencia-publica-para-o-projeto-das-usinas-termeletricas-litos-1-2-3-e-4|title=Ibama realiza audiência pública para o projeto das Usinas Termelétricas Litos 1, 2, 3 e 4|work=IBAMA|access-date=|language=|last=|first=|date=September 29, 2020}}</ref>

As of March 2022, the parent company website offered no updates on the status of the Litos power station.<ref name="ref_B">{{cite web |url=https://litosenergia.com.br/sobre/ |title=Sobre |date= |website=Litos Energia |access-date=2022-03-31 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210512165559/https://litosenergia.com.br/sobre/ |archive-date=May 12, 2021 |url-status=live|last=|first=}}</ref> In September 2021, Litos 1, 2 and 3 were listed as participants in Brazil's A-5 national energy auction<ref name=":02">{{Cite web|url=https://in.gov.br/en/web/dou/-/portaria-n-937/spe/mme-de-14-de-setembro-de-2021-345097967|title=PORTARIA Nº 937/SPE/MME|last=|first=|date=September 14, 2021|website=Ministério de Minas e Energia/Secretaria de Planejamento e Desenvolvimento Energético|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref> but did not emerge victorious.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ccee.org.br/mercado/leilao-mercado|title=Leilões - Agenda e Resultados|last=|first=|date=|website=CCEE|language=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=2022-11-29}}</ref> As of October 2023, Litos continued to be mentioned in press accounts as one of several active power projects in the Macaé region<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://energiaeambiente.org.br/moradores-de-macae-rj-ja-respiram-ar-poluido-e-estao-pressionados-por-novos-projetos-termeletricos-mostra-novo-estudo-do-iema-20211215|title=Moradores de Macaé (RJ) já respiram ar poluído e estão pressionados por novos projetos termelétricos, mostra novo estudo do IEMA|last=|first=|date=2021-12-15|website=Instituto de Energia e Meio Ambiente (IEMA)|language=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://oeco.org.br/reportagens/novas-termeletricas-acendem-o-sinal-de-alerta-em-macae/|title=Novas termelétricas acendem o sinal de alerta em Macaé|last=Emanuel Alencar|first=|date=July 17, 2023|work=((o))eco|access-date=|language=}}</ref>, but it largely dropped out of the news in 2024, leading GEM to conclude that the project had been shelved. 

In September 2025 Litos Energia submitted a formal request to Brazil's environmental agency Ibama for renewal of the preliminary environmental licenses (Licenças Prévias) for all four Litos units.<ref>[https://pesquisa.in.gov.br/imprensa/servlet/INPDFViewer?captchafield=firstAccess&data=02%2F09%2F2025&jornal=530&pagina=252 Litos Energia Ltda: Aviso de Renovação de Licença], Diário Oficial da União, September 2, 2025.</ref> In early May 2026, Ibama granted new 5-year preliminary licenses for all four units, reviving the Litos project's prospects for future development.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2" /><ref name=":3" />

==Opposition==

In November 2022, the Brazilian environmental group Arayara filed a public civil action against Brazil's national environmental authority IBAMA, Rio de Janeiro state environmental authority INEA and four private companies. The filing alleged that the Litos 1, 2, 3 and 4 power stations, along with several other proposed power plants, would have a detrimental impact on water supply and air quality in the region, and requested that additional environmental studies be undertaken prior to further development of the power plants.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arayara.org/litigancia-climatica-arayara-org-entra-com-novas-acoes-judiciais-contra-complexo-termeletrico-em-macae-rio-de-janeiro/|title=Litigância Climática: ARAYARA.ORG entra com novas Ações Judiciais contra Complexo Termelétrico em Macaé, Rio de Janeiro|last=|first=|date=January 26, 2023|website=Arayara|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://epbr.com.br/ambientalistas-tentam-barrar-na-justica-projetos-termeletricos-em-macae/|title=Ambientalistas tentam barrar na Justiça projetos termelétricos em Macaé|last=|first=|date=November 4, 2022|work=EPBR|access-date=|language=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://climate.law.columbia.edu/news/september-2023-updates-climate-case-charts|title=September 2023 Updates to the Climate Case Charts|last=|first=|date=September 13, 2023|website=Sabin Center for Climate Change Law|language=en|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref>
