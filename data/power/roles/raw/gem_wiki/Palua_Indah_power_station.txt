==Background==

The power station will use two GE 9HA.01 gas turbines, which are hydrogen capable but as December 2022 there were no plans for the plant to transition to using hydrogen.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/ge-wins-order-for-12-gigawatts-of-power-equipment-in-malaysia|title=GE Wins Order for 1.2 Gigawatts of Power Equipment in Malaysia|last=|first=|date=March 24, 2021|website=GE|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=December 12, 2022}}</ref>

The overall construction cost for the power station was reported in March 2021 as MYR (Malaysian Riggitt) 3.3 billion, which converted to US$745 million in March 2025. Three Malaysian banks, the development bank Bank Pembangunan and two commercial banks, Maybank and United Overseas Bank, provided a joint loan facility totalling MYR 2.7 billion, or US$610 million in March 2025. The project sponsors Worldwide Holdings and KEPCO provided the remaining MYR 600 million in equity.<ref>[https://www.bpmb.com.my/bank-pembangunan-to-co-finance-rm3-3b-power-plant-project-in-pulau-indah/ Bank Pembangunan to co-finance RM3.3b power plant project in Pulau Indah], Bank Pembangunan press release, Mar. 29, 2021</ref>
