'''Other names:''' Al Ajban Solar

'''Al Ajban PV solar farm''' (محطة العجبان للطاقة الشمسية) is a solar photovoltaic (PV) farm under construction in Al 'Ajban, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.
