==Background==

The proposed project will be located in Songkhla Province, 750 km south of Bangkok, and will also include infrastructure for LNG supply such as a pipeline and storage tanks. The electricity generated by the power plant will be sold to EGAT for 25 years.<ref name='ref_A'>[http://www.businesskorea.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=61714 http://www.businesskorea.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=61714]</ref>
