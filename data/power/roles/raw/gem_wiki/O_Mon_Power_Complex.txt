'''O Mon Power Complex''' (Nhà Máy Nhiệt Điện Ô Môn) is an operating power station of at least 660-megawatts (MW) in O Mon, Cần Thơ, Vietnam with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.

==Background==

The power station comprises four power plants. The O Mon 1 plant, which has a capacity of 660 MW, is already operational. O Mon 2, 3, and 4 are proposed.

O Mon I currently relies on fuel oil but will be switched to domestic natural gas in 2021.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.thesaigontimes.vn/164876/nhiet-dien-o-mon-i-se-chuyen-sang-chay-khi.html|title=Nhiệt điện Ô Môn I sẽ chuyển sang chạy khí|last=|first=|date=September 21, 2017|website=|url-status=live|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=}}</ref> The February 2021 draft PDP8 revised that year to 2024.<ref name='draftPDP8'>[https://moit.gov.vn/upload/2005517/20210707/du-thao-dien-viii35c6.rar Draft National Power Development Plan for the period of 2021-2030, with a vision to 2045], Vietnamese Ministry of Ministry of Industry and Trade, February 2021</ref>

In May 2019, ''VietnamNet'' reported that EVN was having difficulty arranging capital for this project.<ref name='vn'>Hà Duy, [https://vietnamnet.vn/vn/kinh-doanh/dau-tu/bo-cong-thuong-muon-giao-nhiet-dien-o-mon-2-cho-tu-nhan-lam-535091.html Nhiệt điện 26 ngàn tỷ: Chê quốc doanh yếu tiền, Bộ chuyển tư nhân làm], ''VietnamNet'', 29 May 2019</ref>

The power station has three proposed gas-burning combined cycle units totaling 3,150 MW, and the fuel-oil units are scheduled to be switched to natural gas, which would ultimately bring the total gas-fired capacity to 3,810 MW.

==O Mon 2==

O Mon 2 is a proposed 1,050-megawatt (MW) gas-fired power plant. It will be built in the O Mon Power Center alongside O Mon 1 Thermal Power Plant and the proposed O Mon 3 and O Mon 4 power stations.<ref name=sum>Thứ Ba, [http://nangluongvietnam.vn/news/vn/dien-luc-viet-nam/nhiet-dien-o-mon-1-se-chuyen-sang-chay-khi-thay-dau-fo.html Nhiệt điện Ô Môn 1 sẽ chuyển sang chạy khí thay dầu FO], ''Vietnam Energy Magazine'', 22 Sep 2017</ref><ref name=dau>Thanh Hương, [https://baodautu.vn/bo-cong-thuong-lung-tung-khi-duoc-giao-chi-dinh-chu-dau-tu-du-an-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-d108665.html Bộ Công thương lúng túng khi được giao chỉ định chủ đầu tư Dự án Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II], ''Đầu tư online'', 8 Oct 2019</ref>

Two investors sent proposals to the Ministry of Industry and Trade -- EVNGENCO2 (Vietnam Electricity Corporation) and Vietracimex (Marubeni Corporation) to build the power plant. In May 2019, Ministry of Industry and Trade recommended Vietracimex Joint Venture - Marubeni Group, which had begun operating many projects successfully in 2019, to the Prime Minister.<ref name=mon2>[https://baodauthau.vn/dau-tu/du-an-nha-may-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-nha-dau-tu-nao-lot-mat-xanh-bo-cong-thuong-96897.html Dự án Nhà máy Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II: Nhà đầu tư nào “lọt mắt xanh” Bộ Công Thương?], ''Báo Đấu Thầu'', 3 May 2019</ref><ref>[https://thoidai.com.vn/vi-sao-vietracimex-duoc-tien-cu-cho-du-an-khung-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-78041.html Vì sao Vietracimex được tiến cử cho dự án khủng Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II?], ''Vietnam Times'', 24 May 2019</ref>

In mid-2019, the Prime Minister and Ministry of Trade discussed increasing the output of the plant from 750 MW to 1,050 MW and having it begin operations in 2025.<ref name=dau>Thanh Hương, [https://baodautu.vn/bo-cong-thuong-lung-tung-khi-duoc-giao-chi-dinh-chu-dau-tu-du-an-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-d108665.html Bộ Công thương lúng túng khi được giao chỉ định chủ đầu tư Dự án Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II], ''Đầu tư online'', 8 Oct 2019</ref><ref>Vĩnh Chi, [https://vietnamfinance.vn/du-an-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-bo-cong-thuong-che-evngenco2-yeu-nang-luc-tai-chinh-20180504224223635.htm Dự án Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II: Bộ Công Thương chê EVNGENCO2 yếu năng lực tài chính], ''Vietnam Finance'', 15 May 2019</ref>

In February 2021, ''IJGlobal'' reported that Vietracimex and Marubeni had received approval to proceed with the project following a decision by Vietnam's prime minister Nguyen Xuan Phuc in January 2021 backing the investment planning for the project.<ref>[https://baodautu.vn/vay-24448-ty-dong-dau-tu-du-an-nhiet-dien-o-mon-ii-d136037.html Vay 24.448 tỷ đồng đầu tư Dự án Nhiệt điện Ô Môn II], Dautu Online, Jan 5, 2021</ref> The output of the project was confirmed as 1,050 MW and the companies are expecting the plant to commence operations in 2024-2025. The total project cost is about 30.56 trillion Vietnamese dong (US$1.33 billion). The Vietracimex - Marubeni consortium is planning to raise equity of approximately 6.11 trillion Vietnamese dong and finance the remaining 24.45 trillion Vietnamese dong with commercial loans.<ref name=IJG>Civi Yap, [https://ijglobal.com/articles/153133/vietracimex-and-marubeni-to-build-gas-fired Vietracimex and Marubeni to build gas-fired], IJGlobal, Feb. 11, 2021</ref>

As of the February 2021 draft PDP8, O Mon 2 has established a feasibility study.<ref name='draftPDP8'>[https://moit.gov.vn/upload/2005517/20210707/du-thao-dien-viii35c6.rar Draft National Power Development Plan for the period of 2021-2030, with a vision to 2045], Vietnamese Ministry of Ministry of Industry and Trade, February 2021</ref>

In May 2025, it was reported that a Maurbeni-led team was sounding out banks and export credit agencies to raise approximately US$800 million in loans for O Mon 2, with the aim of finalising the finance deals in 2026.<ref name=proximo>Max Thompson, [https://www.proximoinfra.com/news/70425/O-Mon-2-CCGT-out-to-banks O Mon 2 CCGT out to banks], Proximo, May 12, 2025</ref>

==O Mon 3==

O Mon 3 is a proposed 1,050-megawatt (MW) gas-fired power plant in Can Tho Province, Vietnam.<ref name=sum>Thứ Ba, [http://nangluongvietnam.vn/news/vn/dien-luc-viet-nam/nhiet-dien-o-mon-1-se-chuyen-sang-chay-khi-thay-dau-fo.html Nhiệt điện Ô Môn 1 sẽ chuyển sang chạy khí thay dầu FO], ''Vietnam Energy Magazine'', 22 Sep 2017</ref><ref name=jp>[https://www.meti.go.jp/meti_lib/report/H30FY/000209.pdf Study on Gas to Power Project Value Chain in the South Vietnam Final Report, p. 6-45], Japan Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry website, March 2019</ref><ref name=raise>[https://vietnamnet.vn/vn/kinh-doanh/dau-tu/lo-gia-dien-tang-khi-dau-tu-nhiet-dien-o-mon-iii-va-o-mon-iv-456123.html Xây 2 nhiệt điện 50.000 tỷ đồng: Thận trọng những thách thức], ''VietnamNet'', 13 Jun 2018</ref>

==O Mon 4==

O Mon 4 is a proposed 1,050-megawatt (MW) gas-fired power plant.<ref name=sum>Thứ Ba, [http://nangluongvietnam.vn/news/vn/dien-luc-viet-nam/nhiet-dien-o-mon-1-se-chuyen-sang-chay-khi-thay-dau-fo.html Nhiệt điện Ô Môn 1 sẽ chuyển sang chạy khí thay dầu FO], ''Vietnam Energy Magazine'', 22 Sep 2017</ref><ref name=jp>[https://www.meti.go.jp/meti_lib/report/H30FY/000209.pdf Study on Gas to Power Project Value Chain in the South Vietnam Final Report, p. 6-45], Japan Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry website, March 2019</ref><ref name=raise>[https://vietnamnet.vn/vn/kinh-doanh/dau-tu/lo-gia-dien-tang-khi-dau-tu-nhiet-dien-o-mon-iii-va-o-mon-iv-456123.html Xây 2 nhiệt điện 50.000 tỷ đồng: Thận trọng những thách thức], ''VietnamNet'', 13 Jun 2018</ref>

In 2011, following an extensive due diligence, project design, and public consultation period over several years, the Asian Development Bank (ADB) approved a loan totaling US$309.89 million for O Mon 4. The ADB's project cost figure of US$423.45 million for O Mon 4 dates back to 2011.<ref name=ADB>[https://www.adb.org/projects/43400-013/main Viet Nam : O Mon IV Combined Cycle Power Plant Project], Asian Development bank website, accessed May 12, 2025</ref>

In September 2019, the People's Committee of Can Tho City requested comments on the feasibility study report of O Mon IV Thermal Power Plant project from the State Capital Management Committee.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2nksyGwRRhQ Cần Thơ kiến nghị phê duyệt Nhà máy nhiệt điện Ô Môn IV], ''THTPCT Can Tho Television'', 25 Sep 2019</ref>

In June 2025, South Korea's Doosan Enerbility and Vietnam's Power Engineering Consulting Joint Stock Company 2 (PECC2) signed, as a consortium, an engineering, procurement, and construction contract for O Mon 4. Doosan announced that the contract was worth approximately 900 billion won (about US$770 million), with the consortium responsible for main plant equipment supply, construction, and overall commissioning.<ref>Lee Jun-sung, [https://www.koreaittimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=142191 Doosan Enerbility Expands Global Gas-Fired Power Plant Construction Orders], Korea IT Times, Jun. 10, 2025</ref>
