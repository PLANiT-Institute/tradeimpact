'''Shanxi Liulin power station''' (山西国际能源柳林发电厂) is an operating power station of at least 1400-megawatts (MW) in Xuecun Town, Liulin, Lüliang, Shanxi, China.
It is also known as Liulin Liansheng power station, Liulin Huaguang power station 柳林华光热电厂 (Phase II Unit 3, Phase II Unit 4), Liulin Huaguang power station (Phase I Unit 1, Phase I Unit 2).

==Background==

Shanxi Liulin power station originally consisted of four units totaling 1400 MW. The first two units of 100 MW each were put into operation in 1995 and 1996, respectively, at a total investment of 1.1 billion yuan. The units were funded through loans from the Asian Development Bank (ADB).<ref>[http://en.sxgjny.com/llfd/SubComInfo.aspx?comId=55&catalogId=80&tp=Pro&SubCatalogID=22&menuid=1 "Shanxi Liulin Power Generation Co. Ltd,"] Shanxi International Energy Group website, accessed October 2014</ref><ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20230721125406/https://www.mee.gov.cn/gkml/sthjbgw/spwj1/201408/t20140825_288226.htm 关于山西柳林联盛煤矸石综合利用电厂二期工程], mee.gov.cn, 2014-08-20</ref>

In 2008 two more units of 600 MW each began operation.<ref>[http://www.sxgjdl.com/english/history/ "Development History,"] Shanxi International Energy Group website, accessed October 2014</ref>

New units were also built at the related [[Liulin Liansheng power station]].
