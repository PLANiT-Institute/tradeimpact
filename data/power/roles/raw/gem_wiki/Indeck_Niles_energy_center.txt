==Background==

The plant operates with [https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/indeck-niles-energy-center-adds-over-1-gigawatt-of-power-utilizing-advanced-lower GE 7HA.02 turbines] which can burn 20% hydrogen and while there were no plans as of 2022 the possibility exists to modify the turbines to 100% hydrogen within a decade.
