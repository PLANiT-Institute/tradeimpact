'''Tabalong power station''' is an operating power station of at least 200-megawatts (MW) in Adaro's Tanjung coal mine, Tabalong, Kalimantan, Indonesia.
It is also known as Kalsel FTP2.

==Background==

In October 2014, PT Tanjung Power Indonesia signed a power purchase agreement with [[PLN]] for the construction of a two-unit, 200-MW coal-fired power plant in South Kalimantan Province. Tanjung Power, the project's developer, is a joint venture between [[Adaro Indonesia]] and Korea East-West Power (a subsidiary of Korea Electric Power Corporation, or [[KEPCO]]). The plant would be located near Adaro's [[Tabalong coal concession]], and would cost $450-550 million. The project is part of Phase II of Indonesia's Fast Track Program, and would be built under a Build, Own, Operate, & Transfer (BOOT) agreement with PLN. Financing is scheduled to be finalized by late 2015, and the plant is scheduled for completion in 2019.<ref>[http://bisnisuntung7.blogspot.com/2014/10/pltu-2-x-100-mw-kabupaten-tabalong.html PLTU 2 x 100 MW Kabupaten Tabalong Kalsel Akan Dibangun], Bisnis Energi, 17 Oct. 2014.</ref><ref>[http://energitoday.com/2014/11/28/adaro-bangun-pltu-2x100-mw-di-tabalong-us-550-juta/ Adaro Bangun PLTU 2×100 MW di Tabalong US$ 550 Juta], Energi Today, 28 Nov. 2014.</ref><ref>[http://www.pln.co.id/blog/pln-tandatangani-kesepatakan-dengan-dua-pengembang-swasta/ "PLN Tandatangani Kesepatakan Dengan Dua Pengembang Swasta,"] October 15, 2014</ref>

In January 2017, it was reported that construction of the plant was 30% complete.<ref>[https://finance.detik.com/energi/3404026/pembangunan-pltu-tabalong-2x100-mw-di-kalsel-capai-30 Pembangunan PLTU Tabalong 2x100 MW di Kalsel Capai 30%], ''Detik Finance'', Jan. 24, 2017</ref> In August 2017 a building on the construction site collapsed and injured 7 workers.<ref>[https://kumparan.com/@kumparannews/bangunan-pltu-di-kalimantan-selatan-ambruk-7-orang-terluka Bangunan PLTU di Kalimantan Selatan Ambruk, 7 Orang Terluka] ''Kumparan'', Aug. 21, 2017</ref> Construction was expected to be complete in Q3 of 2018.<ref>[http://www.jsbmarketresearch.com/construction/tpi-kalsel-coal-fired-power-plant-200-mw-south-kalimantan-project-profile TPI – Kalsel Coal-Fired Power Plant 200 MW – South Kalimantan - Project Profile
], JBS Market Research, Mar. 26, 2018</ref> In September 2018 it was reported that construction of the plant was 85% complete.<ref>[https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/adaro-energy-adro-pastikan-proyek-pltu-bakal-rampung-tepat-waktu Adaro Energy (ADRO) pastikan proyek PLTU bakal rampung tepat waktu], Kontan, Sep. 23, 2018</ref> In June 2019 Adaro announced that the plant would be commissioned in the second half of 2019.<ref>[https://katadata.co.id/berita/2019/06/28/adaro-pastikan-pltu-tanjung-beroperasi-tahun-ini Adaro Pastikan PLTU Tanjung Beroperasi Tah], Katadata, Jun. 28, 2019</ref> Unit 1 was commissioned in September 2019 and Unit 2 was commissioned in December 2019.<ref>[http://www.adaro.com/pages/read/7/24/Ketenagalistrikan Adaro Power Making Progress Downstream], Adaro, accessed November 2020</ref>

In October 2022, the Institute for Essential Services Reform listed Tabalong power station as a strong candidate for early retirement.<ref>[https://databoks.katadata.co.id/datapublish/2022/10/21/ini-daftar-pltu-yang-layak-pensiun-dini-pada-2023-menurut-iesr Ini Daftar PLTU yang Layak Pensiun Dini pada 2023 menurut IESR], Katadata, October 21, 2022</ref>

In May 2024, PT Tanjung Power Indonesia signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Tabalong Regency Government for the co-firing of municipal waste at their coal-fired power station in Tabalong.<ref>[https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4125738/tabalong-gandeng-swasta-manfaatkan-sampah-organik-bahan-bakar-pltu Tabalong gandeng swasta manfaatkan sampah organik bahan bakar PLTU], Antara News, May 28, 2024</ref>

In July 2025, an article recounted the negative public health impacts attributed to Tabalong power station and [[Tabalong Wisesa power station]].<ref>[https://mongabay.co.id/2025/07/20/pekat-jerubu-pltu-batubara-selimuti-komplek-purnawirawan/ Pekat Jerubu PLTU Batubara Selimuti Komplek Purnawirawan], Mongabay, July 20, 2025</ref>

As of January 2026, the AlamTri Power website stated that Tabalog power station had a 25-year power purchase agreement.<ref>[https://www.alamtri.com/pages/read/7/24/Power AlamTri Power], AlamTri, accessed January 5, 2026</ref>

==Financing==

In October 2015, Adaro Energy secured financing for the proposed plant.<ref>[http://jakartaglobe.beritasatu.com/business/adaro-secures-540m-loan-s-kalimantan-power-project/ "Adaro Secures $540m Loan for S. Kalimantan Power Project,"] Jakarta Globe, October 09, 2015</ref> The project reached financial closure after it secured US$540 million in loans from Korea Development Bank, MUFG Bank, DBS Bank, Mizuho Financial Group, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, and HSBC. The financial advisor for the project is DBS Bank.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://ijglobal.com/data/transaction/32622/kalsel-coal-fired-power-plant-200mw-ipp|title=Preview of KalSel Coal-Fired Power Plant (200MW) IPP {{!}} Transaction {{!}} IJGlobal|website=ijglobal.com|access-date=2020-09-17}}</ref>
