'''Oumache power station''' (محطة كهرباء أوماش) is an operating power station of at least 1456-megawatts (MW) in Oumache, Biskra, Algeria with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as Biskra Combined-Cycle Power Plant, Centrale Oumache I, Centrale Turbine a Gaz de Biskra.
