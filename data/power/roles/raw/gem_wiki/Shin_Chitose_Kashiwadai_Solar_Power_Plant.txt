'''Other names:''' Shin-Chitose Kashiwadai, LSIS Shin Chitose megasolar PV Park, Chitose Solar Power Plant, New Chitose Kashiwadai Solar Power Plant

'''Shin Chitose Kashiwadai Solar Power Plant''' (千歳太陽光発電, 新千歳柏台太陽光発電所) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Chitose, Japan.
