'''Al-Dur Power and Water Station''' (محطة الدور المستقلة للمياه والطاقة، محطة الدورلإنتاج الكهرباء والمياه) is an operating power station of at least 2726-megawatts (MW) in Al Dur, Southern, Bahrain with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as Al Dur2 IWPP (units 2-1, 2-2).
