'''Qurayyah CC power plant''' (مشروع القُريَّة المستقل لإنتاج الكهرباء، محطة كهرباء القرية ، محطة كهرباء القرية المركبة) is an operating power station of at least 4098-megawatts (MW) in Al-Khobar, Eastern, Saudi Arabia with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as Steam Village Station, Hajr for Electricity Production Co. Station, Al-Qurayyah IPP.
