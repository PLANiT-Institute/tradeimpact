==Background==

Trumbull Energy Center is an independent, dual-fuel combined-cycle gas turbine facility with a maximum design capacity of 950 MW, located in Lordstown, Ohio. Operating within PJM's ATSI zone, this power station achieved financial close in November 2023 with a targeted Commercial Operation Date of Jan 31, 2026. Trumbull Energy Center stands on contributions from three equity sponsors: Korea Southern Power Company (KOSPO), Siemens Energy, and Korea Overseas Infrastructure and Urban Development (KIND).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.linkedin.com/company/trumbull-energy-center/|title=Trumbull Energy Center, LLC|website=LinkedIn|url-status=live|access-date=June 12, 2024}}</ref> The first turbine for the project was delivered by Siemens in October 2023.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://businessjournaldaily.com/first-turbine-delivered-to-trumbull-energy-center-site/|title=First Turbine Delivered to Trumbull Energy Center Site|date=November 6, 2023|website=businessjournaldaily.com|url-status=live|access-date=June 12,2 2024}}</ref> In June 2025, it was reported that the Trumbull Energy Center had completed multiple phases of construction and was transitioning to the final phase, which consists of equipment installation, commissioning, and testing.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://dis.puc.state.oh.us/DocumentRecord.aspx?DocID=9477d3fd-a073-463c-9b21-b14b2977a22e|title=Document Record|date=2025-06-27|website=dis.puc.state.oh.us|url-status=live|access-date=2025-11-19}}</ref>
