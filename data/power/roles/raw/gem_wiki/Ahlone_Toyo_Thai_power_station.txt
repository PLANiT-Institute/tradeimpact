'''Ahlone (Toyo Thai) power station''' is an operating power station of at least 121-megawatts (MW) in Ahlone, Yangon, Myanmar with multiple units, some of which are not currently operating.
It is also known as Alone Thermal Power Plant, Toyo Thai power station.
