'''LNG Nghi Son power station''' is a power station in Nghi Sơn, Thanh Hóa, Vietnam with multiple units of varying statuses, none of which are currently operating.
It is also known as LNG Thanh Hoa.

==Background==

The cost of the project has been estimated at US$2.4 billion. Although the bidding process for selecting project investors was cancelled in October 2024, Nhgi Son is one of several proposed thermal plants that are under instruction from Vietnamese Prime Minister Pham Minh Chinh for a construction start before the end of 2025.<ref>[https://vir.com.vn/bidding-cancelled-for-proposed-24-billion-nghi-son-lng-power-plant-116025.html Bidding cancelled for proposed $2.4 billion Nghi Son LNG power plant], Vietnam Investment Review, Oct. 16, 2024</ref><ref>Tri Duc, [https://theinvestor.vn/vietnam-pm-orders-construction-of-2-lng-fired-power-plants-to-begin-in-q2-d14027.html Vietnam PM orders construction of 2 LNG-fired power plants to begin in Q2], The Investor, Jan. 3, 2025</ref>
