==Background==

In June 2015 the leaders of Swaziland and South Korea announced plans for South Korea-based [[KEPCO]] to build a 200 MW IPP coal plant in Swaziland. No other details were provided.<ref>[http://www.observer.org.sz/news/73417-south-korean-company-to-establish-coal-thermal-power.html "South Korean company to establish coal thermal power,"] Swazi Observer, June 5, 2015</ref>

With no further developments, it appears the project is abandoned.
