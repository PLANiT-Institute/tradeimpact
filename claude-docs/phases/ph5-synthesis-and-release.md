# PH5 — Synthesis, policy brief and public release

## Purpose

Close the engagement against its own success criteria (`C-14`): publish everything openly,
state what the three case studies establish and what they do not, map the framework's
implications for GHG Protocol and PCAF standard-setting, and hand the methodology to people
who will use it without the authors. The proposal's own test of success is that "others use
it, adapt it, and build on it" — which requires the outputs to be findable, licensed and
explained, not merely finished.

## Objectives

1. **Synthesis written.** `D-07` states, across the three sectors, what the framework
   established, where it broke, and what residual error each result carries.
2. **Policy brief written.** `D-09` maps TI onto GHG Protocol and PCAF standard-setting,
   including the dual-reporting architecture argument, without claiming adoption (`X-06`).
3. **Everything published openly.** White paper, case studies, dashboard, datasets and code
   under GPL v3, retrievable from a stated location (`C-01`, `C-02`).
4. **Knowledge transfer run.** Training and sharing sessions delivered with materials published
   (`C-10`).
5. **Engagement closed cleanly.** Every deliverable in the charter marked accepted or explicitly
   not delivered, with the reason — no silent shortfall.

- PH1, PH2 and PH4 exit criteria met, or their shortfalls documented.
- All figures reaching the synthesis marked `[verified]`.
- Licence clearance complete for every redistributed source.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] `D-07` and `D-09` published, with retrievable locators recorded.
- [ ] Every charter deliverable has a row in [`../tracker.md`](../tracker.md) §5 reading
      accepted or not delivered with a reason.
- [ ] No number in any published output is unsourced; `provenance-auditor` clearance recorded.
- [ ] Training session materials published (`C-10`).
- [ ] Interpretation language checked: associations and directions, never causal or predictive
      claims (`N-09`), and no netting language anywhere near Scope 3 (`N-01`).
- [ ] Final report to Climate Arc drafted by `consultant` and approved by the project lead
      before it is sent.

## What would invalidate this phase

- **An unsourced figure in a published output.** It breaches `N-03` and `C-02` at once, and the
  affected output is withdrawn rather than annotated.
- **A sector not delivered.** `D-09`'s standard-setting argument rests on cross-sector evidence;
  a single-sector brief is a different, smaller claim and must say so.
