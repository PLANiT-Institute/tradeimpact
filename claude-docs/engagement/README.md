# Engagement — the client-facing record

Owned by `consultant`. Empty at 2026-09-04.

What will live here: drafts of everything that goes to Climate Arc — progress reports on the
proposal's reporting cadence, the `D-05` integration specification cover note, milestone
submissions, and the notes of any call or written exchange. Chronological notes may carry dates in
their filenames; nothing else in `claude-docs/` may.

Two rules that make this directory safe: **drafts only** — nothing here is sent without the project
lead's approval — and **no scope acceptance**. Any Climate Arc request that is not already in
[`../charter.md`](../charter.md) becomes a change-control entry in
[`../log/README.md`](../log/README.md) before it becomes work.

First items expected: resolution of `B-01` (project start date, which fixes every milestone),
`B-05` (whether a countersigned agreement exists) and `B-07` (the P10/P50/P90 commitment in
`C-05`).
